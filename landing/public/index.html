How it works

From conversation, to context, to action.

Meetra does two things. It keeps the team in one place, then it turns that conversation into work.

The workspace

Your team, connected.

Voice, chat, calendar, and the tools you already use stay in one app. A call, a thread, and the work around them become the context.

  • Join a channel in one click. The thread stays next to the call.

  • Calendar, Spotify, Gmail, and Outlook connect without leaving the workspace.

  • Send a conversation to a teammate with the full context attached.
