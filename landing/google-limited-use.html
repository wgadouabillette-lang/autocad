Google API Services User Data Disclosure

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Hall’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Scope of this disclosure

This page explains how Hall accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google user data when you connect Google Calendar and/or Gmail through Hall’s optional connectors. It supplements our Privacy Policy and Subprocessors list.

2. Google user data we access

When you choose to connect a Google account, Hall may access:

  • Google Calendar — calendar metadata and events (read and write) so you can view, create, and manage events from Hall
  • Gmail — message metadata and content as needed to read mail you ask Hall to process, and to send mail on your behalf when you request it
  • Basic account identifiers — such as your Google account email used for OAuth connection status

Access is granted only after you complete Google’s OAuth consent screen and can be revoked at any time in your Google Account permissions or by disconnecting the connector in Hall.

3. How we use Google user data

Hall uses Google user data solely to provide user-facing features you request, including:

  • Displaying and syncing calendar events in Hall
  • Creating, updating, or deleting calendar events you initiate
  • Reading Gmail messages you ask Hall to retrieve or summarize
  • Sending email through Gmail when you explicitly ask Hall to send a message
  • Powering AI features you invoke (for example drafting replies, summarizing threads, or scheduling assistance) using only the Google data needed for that request

4. AI processing

When you use Hall’s AI features with content that includes Google Calendar or Gmail data, that content may be sent to third-party model providers listed on our Subprocessors page (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, or xAI) solely to fulfill your request. Hall does not use Google user data to train generalized AI or machine learning models, and does not sell Google user data.

5. Limited Use commitments

Consistent with Google’s Limited Use requirements, Hall:

  • Does not sell Google user data
  • Does not use Google user data for serving advertisements, or for advertising purposes including retargeting, personalized ads, or ad measurement
  • Does not allow humans to read Google user data unless:
    • we have your affirmative agreement for specific messages;
    • it is necessary for security purposes (e.g. investigating abuse);
    • it is necessary to comply with applicable law; or
    • the data has been aggregated and anonymized for internal operations where those uses comply with Google’s policies
  • Transfers Google user data to third parties only as needed to provide or improve user-facing features you request (for example AI providers processing a prompt you submit), or as required for security/compliance, and not for any other purpose
  • Does not use Google user data for purposes unrelated to providing or improving Hall’s user-facing features

6. Storage and retention

OAuth tokens are stored securely so Hall can call Google APIs on your behalf while the connector remains connected. Message or event content retrieved for a feature may be processed in transit and may appear in chat, notes, or drafts you create in Hall. You can disconnect Google connectors at any time; we then stop new API access and delete or stop using stored tokens in line with our retention practices described in the Privacy Policy.

7. Your choices

  • Connect or disconnect Google Calendar and Gmail from Hall settings
  • Revoke Hall’s access in your Google Account permissions
  • Contact privacy@hall.app for privacy requests related to Google user data
